The story begins

The origin of Floob

  1. A frustrated artist named Zack throws his paintings off the balcony. Two jars of paint, blue and green, smash together on the street.
  2. The next morning there's a one-eyed creature, born from the paint. It laughs, it squeaks, it changes shape.
  3. One day it leaps into one of Zack's landscapes and vanishes into a new land: Chromia.

"Be curious and have fun"

The story

The story so far

Episode 1

A Floob is born

An artist's frustration, two jars of paint and a bolt of lightning. A one-eyed creature is born, and life will never be the same.

Episode 2

Welcome to Chromia

Floob leaps through a painting into a world made of paint. He meets Bonbon, Taffy, Lucky and Pozzo. But how will he get home?

Episode 3

A nemesis appears

Floob makes new friends, but someone is watching from the shadows. Quiddie's jealousy starts a rivalry that could drain all the colour from the land.
