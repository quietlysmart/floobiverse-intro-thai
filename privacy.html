Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Floobiverse Content app is used to manage the FLOOB Creative Instagram account.

What the app does

The app is a private publishing tool used by FLOOB Creative to prepare and publish approved artwork to its own professional Instagram account. It is not a public sign-up service and does not provide accounts for visitors.

Information the app uses

When authorised, the app can receive the Instagram account identifier, account name, account type, approved media, captions and publication results needed to publish and review FLOOB Creative content.

How information is stored and used

Access credentials are stored in a private, access-controlled server environment. Approved artwork and publication records are used only to operate and review the FLOOB Creative content workflow. We do not sell personal information or use this app for advertising audiences.

Sharing

The app sends approved media and captions to Instagram through Meta's official API. It does not share information with other companies except where necessary to host an approved image temporarily for Instagram to fetch during publication.

Retention and removal

Publication records and approved artwork are retained for the FLOOB Creative content archive. If access is revoked, the app can no longer make Instagram API requests. To ask a question about this policy or request removal of information held by the app, contact matt.wills@quietlysmart.ai.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's purpose or data handling changes. The date above will show when the policy was last revised.
